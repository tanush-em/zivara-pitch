# ZIVARA PRODUCT BIBLE

## Definition
Zivara digitizes fragmented local jewellery inventory and connects consumers with shops for discovery, rental and purchase.

## Vision
1. Catalogue
2. Discovery marketplace
3. Rent / Buy / Reserve
4. AI visual search and styling
5. Retailer operating system
6. Marketplace intelligence

## Consumer
Women shopping for weddings, functions, festivals, engagements and receptions. Initial geography: Chennai.

## Retailer
Local jewellery / premium imitation / bridal fashion-jewellery stores with physical inventory and existing offline/WhatsApp workflows.

## Consumer promise
Find beautiful jewellery from local stores without visiting multiple shops blindly.

## Retailer promise
Digitize inventory, generate qualified demand, manage enquiries/bookings, and eventually gain analytics.

## Positioning
Zivara is not primarily an ecommerce store. It is a **visual discovery layer connecting customers with local jewellery inventory.**

## Pillars
- Discovery: Pinterest-like visual browsing
- Marketplace: local shops + rental/purchase
- Looks: complete jewellery combinations
- AI: stylist, visual search, saree matching, natural-language discovery
- Locality: Chennai-first
- Retailer infrastructure: inventory, bookings, enquiries, analytics

## MVP
Include: fake catalogue, shops, search, filters, wishlist, product pages, looks, shops, rental reservation simulation, purchase/enquiry simulation, AI stylist, visual search, saree matcher, retailer dashboard, admin dashboard, fake analytics.

Exclude: real payments, real WhatsApp API, real auth, real inventory sync, real maps, real booking provider, production ML.

## Demo success
Within 30 seconds a viewer should understand what Zivara is, why it is visually different, that it connects consumers to local shops, that jewellery can be rented/bought, that AI assists discovery, and that shops receive a business dashboard.
