# Zivara Image Research

Research log for publicly licensed imagery to seed the Zivara prototype catalogue.

**Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Sources prioritised:** Unsplash, Pexels, Wikimedia Commons  
**Not used:** ecommerce scrapes, Unsplash+, iStock/NurPhoto editorial-only stock, watermarked assets, luxury-brand logo imagery  

**Disclaimer:** Fictional Zivara product/look names are *visual representations* for the demo. Photographed pieces are not claimed to be those exact SKUs unless later confirmed with owned photography.

## Visual coherence strategy

Chosen assets share a warm gold / antique-gold palette, velvet or deep-red display grounds for product heroes, and South-Asian bridal/editorial styling for looks.

1. **Product heroes** — Prefer studio-style jewellery on velvet/black/red grounds (Zayed Ahmed Unsplash series + Pexels bridal product displays + Wikimedia product stills). Avoid mixed Western fine-jewellery aesthetics.
2. **Complete looks** — Prefer Indian bridal / saree portraits with visible jewellery (necklace, jhumkas, bangles, tikka). Lean Chennai / South Indian where available.
3. **Editorial** — Guest saree styling, henna + jewellery details, Kanchipuram-adjacent silk styling.
4. **Shops** — Jewellery case displays, bangle stands, showroom trays — no identifiable third-party retail brands.

**Excluded after review:** Unsplash+ / premium-locked images, elephant-jewellery novelty shots, low-res stamp scans, Grand Bazaar non-Indian context where mismatched, watermarked ecommerce, and any frame with visible luxury logos.

## Coherence review (pre-integration)

**Verdict:** Catalogue is visually coherent enough for the prototype.

- **Palette:** Dominated by warm gold metal, deep red/velvet grounds, and silk bridal colourways (red, maroon, green, purple, gold).
- **Photography style:** Product lane leans studio velvet (Zayed series + Pexels red-ground displays). Look/editorial lane leans South Asian bridal portraiture with several Chennai-tagged Pexels assets — good for the Chennai-first pitch.
- **Quality bar:** All primary image URLs returned HTTP 200 at research time; Wikimedia files are multi-megapixel; Unsplash/Pexels heroes are HD.
- **Risks / follow-ups:**
  1. Wikimedia CC BY-SA assets need visible attribution in credits or about page for public release.
  2. A few product heroes share source photos with different crops (ZV005/ZV025) — intentional for set vs necklace; consider replacing one before production.
  3. Some “temple jewellery” representation is stylistic (gold traditional), not always Motif-of-Lakshmi temple carving — replace with owned temple-jewellery photography when pilot shops onboard.
  4. Re-check Unsplash pages that were bot-gated during research before shipping hotlinks; prefer downloading into `/assets` for stability.
  5. Avoid implying photographed items are inventory of fictional shops.
